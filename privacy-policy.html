Privacy Policy

WordLink — Word Chain Puzzle Game

Effective date: June 17, 2026  •  Last updated: June 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WordLink ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS application. WordLink is built to be private by design: we collect only the minimum data needed to run the game, and we never ask for your name, email, or any other personal information.

1. No Personal Information Collected

WordLink does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information. You can play the entire game without ever providing personal details.

2. Anonymous Device ID

To remember your progress, the app creates an anonymous identifier on first launch using Supabase anonymous authentication. This identifier is stored on your device and reused on that device. It:

  • Contains no personal information
  • Is used only to track which puzzles you have already played, so you are not shown the same puzzle twice
  • Cannot be used to identify you

3. Anonymous Usage Data

WordLink uses Supabase as its backend service. The only data associated with your anonymous identifier is gameplay information such as which puzzles you have completed and the difficulty level you selected. This anonymous usage data is used solely to deliver puzzles and to avoid repeating puzzles you have already completed.

4. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. Supabase is used only as the secure backend infrastructure that stores the anonymous data described above on our behalf.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Microphone or camera data

6. Children's Privacy

WordLink does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no advertising and no social features.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
