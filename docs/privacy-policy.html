Privacy Policy

WordLink — Word Chain Puzzle Game

Effective date: June 18, 2026  •  Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WordLink ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS application. WordLink is built to be private by design: version 1 can be played without an account, without advertising, and without providing your name, email, or other personal details.

1. No Account Required

WordLink does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or other contact information. You can play the app without providing personal details.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

WordLink stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can provide core features such as history and progress. This may include:

  • Puzzles you have played
  • Your selected difficulty
  • Game results and history
  • Basic app preferences

This local information stays on your device unless a backend feature described below is enabled.

3. Optional Backend Services

WordLink may use Supabase as backend infrastructure for puzzle delivery, anonymous progress support, or related app features. When these backend features are enabled, Supabase may process limited technical and gameplay information needed to provide the service, such as an anonymous app identifier, selected difficulty, completed puzzle records, and standard network request information.

These backend features do not require a personal account and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. No Ads, Tracking, or Sales of Data

Version 1 of WordLink does not include ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Payment information

6. Children's Privacy

WordLink does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no advertising and no social features.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
