Privacy Policy

WordLink — Word Chain Puzzle Game

Effective date: May 2, 2025  •  Last updated: May 2, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how WordLink ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to collecting the minimum data necessary to operate the game.

1. No Personal Information Collected

WordLink does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. The app is anonymous by design.

2. Anonymous Session Identifier

On first launch, the app generates a random anonymous user ID via Supabase anonymous authentication. This ID:

  • Contains no personal information
  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is used solely to track which puzzles you have already played, so you are not shown the same puzzle twice
  • Is never linked to your identity

3. Game Play Data

When you play a game, the following minimal data is sent to our backend (Supabase):

  • Your anonymous session ID
  • The selected difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard)
  • Your correct word guesses during a session

This data is used only to serve you unseen puzzles and has no connection to your identity.

4. Game History (Stored Locally)

Your game history (scores, completed chains, dates) is stored only on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is limited to your most recent 20 games
  • Can be cleared at any time by deleting the app

5. Puzzle Generation

Puzzles are pre-generated and stored in the app. In cases where a new puzzle is generated on-demand, a request containing only the difficulty level is sent to OpenAI's API. No personal data is included in these requests.

6. Audio

The app uses iOS text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to pronounce words aloud. This processing happens entirely on-device. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

7. Third-Party Services

WordLink uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – Backend database and anonymous authentication. Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI – On-demand puzzle generation (difficulty level only). Privacy Policy

8. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • Advertising or tracking data
  • Crash reports or analytics
  • Microphone recordings

9. Children's Privacy

WordLink does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or social features.

10. Data Retention

Anonymous session data on our servers is retained to track puzzle history and prevent repetition. Because sessions are anonymous, we have no way to identify or delete data belonging to a specific person upon request. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new anonymous ID and effectively starts fresh.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
